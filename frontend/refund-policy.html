Cancellation, Return and Refund Policy

This policy explains how cancellation, return, and refund requests are handled for purchases from Vansh Computer.

Change-of-Mind Returns

Returns are not accepted for change of mind or personal preference.

Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Products

If a laptop arrives damaged, defective, not working as described, or different from the item ordered, customers can contact Vansh Computer with their order details and supporting photos or videos. Each request will be reviewed, and an appropriate remedy will be provided in accordance with applicable consumer protection laws.

Return Request

Customers should contact the store as soon as possible after delivery to report a damaged, defective, or incorrect product. Return or replacement requests are subject to verification and applicable legal requirements.

Order Cancellation

Cancellation requests depend on the order's processing and dispatch status. Eligible cancellations of prepaid orders will be handled according to the applicable refund rules.

Refund Processing

Once a refund is approved, it will be initiated within 5–7 business days. The time taken for the amount to appear in the customer's account may vary depending on the payment method and bank.

Return Shipping

Return instructions and any applicable shipping charges will be communicated to the customer before a return is arranged.

Contact Information

Customers can contact Vansh Computer through the contact details already available on the website.
